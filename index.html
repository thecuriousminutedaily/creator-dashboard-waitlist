Questions

Is it free?

Early access is free. If a paid version comes later, waitlist members get the best deal.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 laptop and a YouTube channel. Mac support is on the list; tell us in the form if you need it.

Is my channel data safe?

The dashboard runs on your own computer and only reads your stats. It can't post, delete or change anything, and your keys are never uploaded anywhere.

Does it work with Instagram?

Yes, but Meta's setup takes longer, so the Instagram add-on comes after the YouTube starter kit.

Do I need to be monetised?

No. It's built for growing channels too, and it shows exactly how close you are to the Partner Program.
